Aura

Emotion Tracker Journal — Privacy Policy

Short version

Your moods, notes and tags stay on your phone. Aura has no accounts, no cloud, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting SDKs. The app does not request the Internet permission.

Data the app stores

What you enter: mood check-ins with their date and time, optional notes and tags, your own tags, reminder times and app settings.

  • Stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • Never sent to the developer or any third party.
  • Patterns and insights are calculated on your device.
  • Deleted with “Delete all data” in Settings, or when you uninstall the app.

Aura lock

If you turn on Aura lock, the app asks Android to verify you with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Biometric data is handled by Android and never reaches the app.

Sharing, saving and backups

  • Images (a year in pixels, aura of the month), CSV files and backups leave the app only when you choose to share or save them, to the app or folder you pick.
  • Android's own system backup may include the app's database and settings if you enabled it on your phone; this is controlled by you and Google, not by the app.

Notifications and widgets

  • Check-in reminders are scheduled locally on your device. On the lock screen they are shown without text.
  • Home screen widgets read data from the app on your device only.

Purchases

Aura Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. Payment details are seen only by Google. The app only checks whether Pro was bought.

Not a medical app

Aura is a personal journal for self-reflection. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment or medical advice.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data.
